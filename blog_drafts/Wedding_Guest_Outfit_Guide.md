## What to Wear to an Indian Wedding: The Ultimate Guest Guide

So, you’ve received an invitation to an Indian wedding! Indian weddings are famous for their vibrant colors, rich traditions, and multi-day celebrations. If you are based in Germany or Europe and wondering how to navigate the cultural dress code, you are in the right place.

At **Neha Couture**, we specialize in dressing guests for these exact occasions. Here is your ultimate guide on what to wear to an Indian wedding, broken down by the most common events.

---

### 1. The Haldi & Mehendi Ceremonies (Daytime, Pre-Wedding)
These are joyous, pre-wedding ceremonies usually held during the daytime. The Haldi involves applying turmeric paste to the couple, while the Mehendi is the henna ceremony.

* **The Vibe:** Fun, colorful, and comfortable.
* **Colors to Wear:** Yellow, Mustard, Green, Peach, and bright pastels.
* **What to Wear (Women):** A vibrant [Kurti Set](/collections/kurti), a lightweight [Indo-Western Fusion Outfit](/collections/western-collection-with-indian-flavour), or a simple flowing skirt with a crop top. Comfort is key because you will likely be dancing and sitting for henna application!
* **What to Wear (Men):** A smart and simple [Cotton Kurta Pajama](/collections/mens) in bright shades like yellow or green.

### 2. The Sangeet (Evening, Pre-Wedding)
The Sangeet is the musical night. Expect a lot of choreographed dancing, music, and a glamorous party atmosphere.

* **The Vibe:** Glamorous, glittering, and ready to dance.
* **What to Wear (Women):** This is the perfect night for a flared [Lehenga Choli](/collections/indian-wedding-clothes-in-germany) or a stunning, twirl-worthy [Sharara Suit](/collections/handwork-ladies-suits-in-germany). Look for fabrics that flow nicely when you spin, accented with mirror work or sequins.
* **What to Wear (Men):** A Silk Kurta paired with a tailored Nehru Jacket from our [Mens Collection](/collections/mens) will keep you looking sharp on the dance floor.

### 3. The Main Wedding Ceremony (Day or Evening)
This is the religious ceremony where the couple ties the knot. 

* **The Vibe:** Traditional, modest, and highly respectful. 
* **Colors to Avoid:** It is generally polite to avoid wearing plain Black or plain White to the religious ceremony. Additionally, avoid wearing heavy Red or Maroon, as those are traditionally reserved for the Bride.
* **What to Wear (Women):** A classic [Saree](/collections/buy-saree-in-germany) or an elegant, heavy [Anarkali Suit](/collections/ladies-suits-in-germany). If you are nervous about draping a saree, a heavy Anarkali or a pre-stitched saree is a stress-free, beautiful alternative. Make sure to carry your dupatta, as you may be asked to cover your head during the religious rites.
* **What to Wear (Men):** A regal Sherwani or a heavily embroidered [Designer Kurta](/collections/mens). 

### 4. The Reception (Evening, Post-Wedding)
The Reception is the grand finale—a massive party to celebrate the newly married couple.

* **The Vibe:** Black-tie elegance meets Bollywood glamour.
* **What to Wear (Women):** Bring out your heaviest, most glamorous outfits. An opulent [Party Wear Gown](/collections/partywear), a dark velvet Lehenga, or a shimmering Georgette Saree. Pair it with striking [Indian Jewellery](/collections/jewellery). 
* **What to Wear (Men):** A western tuxedo or suit is completely acceptable here. Alternatively, a dark, well-tailored Indo-Western jacket.
