Quick Answer (TL;DR): For an Indian wedding in Europe, dress by event: (1) Haldi/Mehendi: Bright yellow or pastel Kurtis & lightweight skirts; (2) Sangeet: Sparkling Lehengas or twirl-friendly Shararas for women, Kurta & Nehru jacket for men; (3) Main Ceremony: Traditional Silk Sarees or rich Anarkali suits (avoid plain black, white, and bridal red); (4) Reception: Glamorous Partywear Gowns, velvet Lehengas, or tuxedo/Indo-Western suits.

Received an invitation to an Indian wedding? Prepare for a dazzling celebration filled with joyous music, heartwarming rituals, delicious cuisine, and breathtaking multi-day celebrations. 1. The Haldi & Mehendi (Daytime, Pre-Wedding)

The Haldi involves applying auspicious turmeric paste to the couple, while Mehendi is the joyous henna application. Both are festive, interactive daytime events.

  • The Dress Code Vibe: Cheerful, colorful, and comfortable.
  • Colors to Wear: Yellow, Mustard, Mango, Sage Green, and playful pastels.
  • Women's Outfits: A breezy Festive Kurti Set, a lightweight Indo-Western Tunic, or a simple flared skirt with a crop top. Comfort is paramount since you will be seated for henna application.
  • Men's Outfits: A crisp, comfortable Cotton Kurta Pajama in mustard, lime, or peach.

2. The Sangeet (Evening, Musical Night)

The Sangeet is an energetic dance party. Expect high-energy Bollywood music, choreographed performances, and an electric atmosphere.

  • The Dress Code Vibe: Glamorous, shimmering, and dance-friendly.
  • Women's Outfits: A twirl-worthy Lehenga Choli or a flared Sharara Suit with mirror work, sequins, or metallic threadwork that catches the stage lights.
  • Men's Outfits: A tailored silk kurta layered with an embroidered Nehru waistcoat from our Men's Festive Attire.

3. The Main Wedding Ceremony (Day or Evening)

This is the sacred religious ceremony where the couple takes their vows around the sacred fire (Pheras).

  • The Dress Code Vibe: Traditional, elegant, and culturally respectful.
  • Etiquette Note: Avoid wearing completely plain black or white (traditionally associated with mourning). Also, avoid all-red or deep scarlet, which is traditionally reserved for the bride.
  • Women's Outfits: A classic Banarasi Silk Saree or an opulent floor-length Anarkali Suit. Always keep a matching dupatta handy to respectfully cover your head if entering the Mandap or temple.
  • Men's Outfits: A structured Bandhgala suit or an ornate embroidered silk Kurta set.

4. The Grand Reception (Evening Finale)

The grand concluding party celebrating the newlyweds with speeches, formal dinner, and dancing.

  • The Dress Code Vibe: Black-tie elegance meets modern Bollywood luxury.
  • Women's Outfits: Your most opulent ensemble: a regal velvet lehenga, a contemporary sequin saree, or an Indian-inspired Evening Party Gown. Pair it with statement Kundan or Polki Indian Jewellery.
  • Men's Outfits: A dark tuxedo, formal western suit, or structured Indo-Western royal jacket.
